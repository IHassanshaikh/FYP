## Codebase Patterns
- **Expo Framework:** Expo v56 with Expo Router in `app/`.
- **Three.js in WebView/iframe:** The 3D live visualizer runs inside `components/LiveSwingVisualizer.tsx` via Three.js r134.
- **Realistic PBR Graphics:** Tone mapping is `ACESFilmicToneMapping` with exposure 1.25. Textures are procedurally baked onto 2D canvas elements (wood grain willow, cricket pad ribs, jersey weave, matte skin) and mapped onto `MeshStandardMaterial`.
- **Side-On Batting Biomechanics:** Right-handed batsman stands side-on: chest facing off-side (+X), front leg forward towards bowler (+Z) with flexed knee, rear leg back across stumps (-Z), head turned 75° over front shoulder with gaze fixed on bowler.
- **Protective Equipment:** Pro 7-cane bolster pads with knee wings; pro multi-segment finger roll batting gloves gripping handle in textbook two-handed 'V' grip; carbon composite helmet shell with StemGuard rear neck protector and double-bar titanium face grille.
- **Stadium & Pitch:** Procedural clay-loam pitch with crease markings and footmark scuffs; radial concentric lawn mowing stripes; 4 steel floodlight towers with halo lens flares.
- **AI Shot Prediction:** `services/AiShotPredictionService.ts` integrates Grok beta API as primary with Gemini 1.5 Flash fallback.
- **Hardware Integration:** ESP32 WebSocket server at `ws://192.168.1.11:81`. When disconnected, no hardcoded swing data is simulated (OFFLINE badge).
